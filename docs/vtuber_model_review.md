# VTuber model review: composition and eyebrows (2026-11-01, references viewed 2026-11-02)

Owner feedback on the 15-frame expression sheet (`art/generated/vtuber2/final/`): the design is fine, but the
composition and the eyebrows have problems; look at Japanese illustrations of cute, cheeky (生意気) young girls
with smaller eyebrows, then analyse the sheet frame by frame.

**Method.** After the owner allowed the Japanese domains, these were visited directly:
- **pixivision** illustration features (preview images, 433 in total, kept in the session scratchpad only; not
  committed, not redistributed): ドヤ顔 2807 / 5739 / 6602 / 7794 / 10318 / 11320, ジト目 1121 / 3186 / 5296 / 6853,
  ふくれっ面 408 / 4161 / 7817, 眉毛 1359 / 9688, 八重歯 6110 / 9858 / 10861, 猫口 11459, 萌え袖 5239 / 6349 / 7255,
  上目遣い 10644 / 11668, ウィンク 10781 / 11982, 赤面 10846, 猫耳 10102 / 10850, VTuber fan art 9476 / 11724.
  Each was viewed as contact sheets, and 32 were cropped to the face for brow study.
- Guide articles read in full: お絵かき図鑑 1448 (children) and 44434 (expressions), MediBang (ドヤ顔).

Not reached: dic.pixiv.net (403), いちあっぷ (only the site shell came back), clipstudio.net and pref.kochi.lg.jp
(not in the session's allowed domains). pixiv artwork pages were not opened (they need a login); the pixivision

## 1. What the references show
### 1a. Eyebrows (observed in the illustrations)
- **Short, thin, tapered, dark.** In every smug / pout / brow close-up the brow is a short stroke, about as wide as
  the eye or less, in the dark hair-outline tone (darker than the hair fill), thinning at the tail
  (smug05, smug66, smug74, pout15, brows09, brows15, catmouth11, vtuber20).
- **Close to the eye.** It sits just above the upper lid, about half an eye height or less (visual estimate from
  the crops), never far up on the forehead.
- **Drawn over the bangs (透け眉).** With long bangs the brow is still drawn on top of the hair, so it reads through
  it (smug05, smug66, pout15, brows09, catmouth11, vtuber20).
- **Calm faces still have brows.** In calm cute faces with long, heavy bangs (catears04, catears13, sleeves06,
  vtuber20) the brows are faint but present: a short soft stroke just above each eye.
- **The brow carries the emotion.**
  - Smug: つり眉, narrowed eyes, then :3 or one mouth corner up (smug05, smug55, smug66, catmouth11).
  - Annoyed pout: inner ends pulled down and together, with a pout or puffed cheek (pout12, pout15, brows09).
  - Sulky / embarrassed: ハの字, blush, puffed cheeks, hands on the cheeks (pout01).
- **Lash flicks are normal.** Strong upper lash lines with an outer flick appear in many cute references
  (catears13, upward14, smug45), so her lash flick is not the problem (this corrects the first review).

### 1b. Composition (observed)
- Bust-ups and close-ups crop so the **face fills about a third to a half** of the frame (vtuber09, vtuber21,
  catears04, smug74). Waist-up pictures make up for the smaller face with a **pose**.
- **Hands or sleeves sit near the face.**
  - vtuber04: fingers at the cheeks; vtuber08: cheek on her hand; vtuber11: hand at her glasses.
  - sleeves06: sleeve over the mouth; sleeves12: hands on her hat; pout01: hands on her cheeks.
- **Heads tilt and turn.** The face is in 3/4 view or tilted (smug55, catmouth11, vtuber09, vtuber02). Square-on
  symmetric framing is rare.
- **The neck is mostly hidden** by the collar, scarf or hair. Shoulders are narrow and sleeves long.
- **Backgrounds are light**: flat or graphic, with small doodles, stickers or UI motifs (vtuber04, vtuber06,
  vtuber13). The background is never one dark block.

### 1c. Guide texts (read on the pages)
- お絵かき図鑑 1448 (children): 「細めの眉は、男女ともに可愛らしく幼い印象」; 「丸みのある大きな目・曲線状の眉毛」;
  child 4-5 heads vs adult 6-7; children's expressions are drawn **stronger** than adults' (「眉毛を吊り上げ、
  ほっぺを膨らませて怒っている様子など、大人よりも表情を強調して描くようにすると、より子供らしく」).
- お絵かき図鑑 44434 (expressions): anger: 「眉と目を近づける」, 「眉間に縦ジワ」; surprise: the stretched skin
  「眉から目までの間にある、目一杯伸ばしたスジ」 (brows lifted far from the eyes).
- MediBang (ドヤ顔): 「笑顔の眉をつり眉にするだけでドヤ顔」; cool smug: 「いつもよりやや上にアーチ状の眉」,
  「少しだけつり眉」.
- Search extracts from the first pass (pages not opened) agree: eyes slightly low, wider spacing, short chin and
  neck, round contour (Esolab, note/nigaoe1, art-watage); 困り眉 = ハの字 (Adobe); 透け眉 (アタムアカデミー).

## 2. Whole-sheet findings (measured on her frames)
Measured on the 2048 px base (`final/base.png`). Key heights: crown y 190, eyes 800, chin 1030, collar 1190.
The bottom edge cuts at the shorts waistband.

**Eyebrows: in practice she has none, and they never change** (figure B, C).
- What they are: thin **pale-blue hair-tone outline lines**, not dark strokes.
- Where they sit: about 100 px above the lid on the viewer-left eye (1.5 eye heights) and about 85 px (1 eye
  height) on the viewer-right eye. The references put the brow at about half an eye height or less.
- They are mostly covered by the bangs. At the stream's small-cam scale (the 1024 bust drawn at about 720 px) they
  disappear completely.
- Only eyes / mouth / cheeks were pasted per expression, so no frame changes them. That is the main reason angry,
  shocked, smug and flustered read weak.
- Their **thinness is right** (細めの眉). Their **position, tone and size** are wrong for her age and for expressions.

**The base eyes are already ジト目.**
- Half-lidded eyes with parallel lid lines plus a closed smile read cool / sleepy-smug.
- So neutral and smug look alike. A young base wants rounder, more open eyes and keeps the ジト目 for the smug frame.

**Composition is a cropped reference sheet** (figure A).
- She is centred, square to the camera and symmetric. The arms hang down and are cut at the sides; no hands.
- The frame cuts at the shorts waistband (the button shows).
- 160 px of neck is visible, about a fifth of the head height.
- The navy sweater fills the bottom 40% as one dark block.
- Against the references: nothing near the face, no tilt or 3/4 turn, the neck shows, the shoulders are square.

**Two eye styles are mixed.** Frames 5, 13 and 14 have an N5-drawn open eye that is rounder and sparklier than her
sheet eye; side by side they look like two artists.

**Redundant frames.** cheer / laugh / sing, blink / honest and flustered / frustrated read almost the same.

## 4. Proposed fix (written before the redraw; what was done is in section 5)
1. **New framing.** A new N5 bust-up drawn from her sheet so the design stays:
   - from mid-chest up, the face about a third of the frame height, the collar hiding most of the neck;
   - a slight head tilt / 3/4 turn;
   - long sleeves with one hand or sleeve near the face (萌え袖);
   - a light background (the cam box backdrop already supplies her room).
2. **Visible brows.**
   - Short, thin, tapered, dark hair-outline tone, about half an eye height or less above the lid, drawn over the
     bangs.
   - They are a separate part redrawn in every expression, together with the eyes and mouth, as in Live2D 表情差分.
   - Brow shape per expression as in section 3. Prompt tags: `thin eyebrows`, `eyebrows visible through hair`,
     `v-shaped eyebrows`, `raised eyebrow`, `worried eyebrows` (not `short eyebrows`: in V5 that means 麻呂眉
     dots, see section 5).
3. **One eye style** for every open eye, and a rounder neutral eye than today's base.
4. **A tighter set of 12** without duplicates: neutral, blink, talk (a / o), smug, wink, happy, sing, shocked,
   angry, pout / hmph, flustered, pain.
5. **N5 settings.**
   - So far only Opus-included generations were used: normal size, 28 steps, 0 Anlas. That is why the owner
     asked about the free tier.
   - Keeping her design across a new pose and 12 expressions works best with **Precise Reference** (5 Anlas per
     image here before) and benefits from a larger canvas and more steps, which also cost Anlas.
   - A full redo with about 3 takes per frame is roughly 40-60 images, about 200-300 Anlas with Precise Reference
     alone. The balance is 8 Anlas.
   - Buying Anlas is the owner's call. The exact cost of each run is quoted before it starts.

## 5. v3 redraw (2026-11-02)
The owner authorised N5 generation. The balance was 1 Anlas, so Precise Reference and larger canvases were not
available. Every image is a free normal-size V5 img2img (1024x1024, 28 steps, 0 Anlas): 107 takes in 13 rounds,
listed in `art/generated/vtuber3/prompts.sh`. Code: `pixel/vt3.py`. Frames: `art/generated/vtuber3/final/`
(2048 px, cut out by `model3()`).

**Composition.** A paste-up of her sheet sets the framing, and V5 redraws it at 0.62:
- mid-chest crop with the head tilted;
- one oversized sleeve raised to her cheek (萌え袖, as in sleeves06 / vtuber04 / vtuber08);
- the collar and hair hide most of the neck;
- no waistband, no square ID-photo stance.

The chosen take keeps her design: royal-blue hair (mean colour within 7 levels of the sheet), cat ears, cat clip,
navy sailor collar with blue stripes, short tie with the black cat emblem, light-blue cat patch, striped cuffs.
The face reads younger than v2: round open eyes, short face.

**Eyebrows.**
- Painting short brow guides near the eyes did not work (rounds 3, 3b, 6). V5 turned dark strokes over the hair
  into pale hair-clip bars, or hid them under the bangs at higher strength.
- What worked: V5 draws visible brows over the bangs when the expression asks for them (round 4). So every frame
  takes its brows from its own V5 take, inside one band that covers both eyes and brows:
  - smug: つり眉, one brow up;
  - angry and pout: inner ends down (V);
  - shocked: lifted;
  - flustered: ハの字;
  - pain: pinched.
- In "short eyebrows" V5 means round 麻呂眉 dots high on the forehead (round 1). That tag sits in the negative.
- **Limit:** in the neutral face V5 keeps the brows as thin dark lines through the bangs, about one eye height
  above the lash line. That is higher than the references' calm faces. Three prompt variants (rounds 5, 7, 12)
  did not bring them lower without turning neutral into smug.

**Expression set (13).**
- neutral, blink (eyes only, so the brows stay put), talk a / o (mouth only);
- smug, wink, happy, sing, shocked, angry, pout, flustered, pain.
- All open eyes come from the same body, so they share one eye style.
- Removed v2 duplicates: cheer / laugh, honest, frustrated.

**Cleanliness.**
- Alignment and colour: each paste is aligned on a ring around its region (±10 px search). Colours are matched
  separately for skin and for hair / lines (a single fit had pulled the skin grey).
- Seams: a feathered cross-fade of two takes' bangs left half-transparent ghost strands at 2048 px. Each region is
  now cut along a closed path where the two images agree (dynamic programming around the ellipse). A multi-band
  blend then switches line art within about a pixel and blends colour over about 10 px.
- happy and flustered: V5 drew the cat clip on a pin bar in those takes, and the old band picked up a stray piece
  of it. The seam leaves it out.
- blink: the closed-eye takes kept a flat pale wash where the eyes had been painted over. The take's skin is now
  pulled to the neutral frame's skin with a smooth correction measured on the skin around the eyes. Left: a
  2-3 px strand cut at the outer corner of her right eye (viewer's left).
- Cut-out: removes V5's white rim around the outline and the background seen through gaps between strands; keeps
  the white ear fur and eye whites.

**Quota.** After 118 free generations on 2026-11-02 (107 takes plus 11 accidental re-runs of rejected takes), NovelAI started charging Anlas for normal-size V5 (HTTP 402,
13-15 Anlas per image, 1 Anlas left), so the Opus plan's free generations are used up. A planned blink v2 (round 13)
was not run.
