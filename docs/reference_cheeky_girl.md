# Video references for XIYIN: the cheeky small girl (survey, 2026-10-25)

Owner request: survey Japanese anime PVs and VTuber reference characters and find video references that suit XIYIN,
fitting the cheeky young girl / "loli" archetype.

## 0. Scope, boundary, evidence
- **Assumption:** XIYIN is the navy cat-girl OC of this repo (README §1: "acts cool and smug -> trips -> that doesn't
  count -> blushes -> owns it"). Confirm with the owner; nothing here depends on the name.
- **The archetype as used here:** 生意気 (cheeky), 小悪魔 (little devil), ドヤ顔 (smug face) and ギャップ (smug -> flustered),
  childlike in attitude and silhouette. **Never sexualised** (README §1; camera rule in `docs/redesign_v4.md`: no low
  angles on the legs, no thigh crops).
- **Excluded on purpose:** the メスガキ label (its moegirl entry lists sexualised related tags [search]); teasing works led
  by fanservice (e.g. 『イジらないで、長瀞さん』, 『宇崎ちゃんは遊びたい!』); songs with suggestive titles or lyrics; any
  succubus-type persona. What is kept is cheekiness played for comedy and warmth.
- **Evidence labels:** **[read]** page opened this session or earlier in this project; **[search]** seen only through search
  summaries; **[own]** my knowledge of the works, not checked this session. Videos could not be watched or downloaded
  from this environment (YouTube bot check, Bilibili CDN blocked), so every "what to study" note is **[own]** and must be
  checked by watching. Reference videos are **ideas only**: never fed into image/video generation, never copied.

## 4. What this means for production [own]
- The references share **big readable poses with anticipation and holds**, and a **second, chibi body** for gags. Our
  generated clips lacked both (`docs/qa_block64_v42.md` diagnosis: low-energy clips, uneven cadence). Use the
  shortlisted moments as written motion briefs (pose, hold, snap, count), not as input videos.
- XIYIN's design already has two signature props for this archetype: **sleeves past the hands** (follow-through on
  every gesture) and the **tail** (mood signal: up = smug, flick = annoyed, wrapped = shy). Both should act, not sway.
- A real **chibi model sheet** is the single most useful new asset (fail, ノーカン, めっ inserts, Shorts).
