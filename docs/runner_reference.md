Researched 2026-11-01 for the owner's v2 feedback ("the running logic is wrong"; reference Muse Dash and other

## Muse Dash (PeroPeroGames, Unity; mobile 2018, PC/Switch 2019)
- Side-scrolling rhythm action with **two lanes, air and ground**; the Muse **runs constantly**; enemies and
  projectiles **come from the right**. [review: Nintendo World Report, TheSixthAxis, Gaming Trend; Wikipedia]
- **One button per lane**: the ground button brings her to the ground and attacks the ground enemy; the air button
  makes her **jump and attack in the air**. Obstacles on the ground are avoided by jumping (air button). Normal
  characters **fall back down** after an air attack (only flying characters stay up). [wiki: denfaminicogamer;
  review: Gaming Trend]
- Enemy colours: ground enemies red, air enemies blue (in the tutorial colour coding). [wiki extract]
- **Contact = damage**: touching an enemy or obstacle costs HP; HP 0 = game over. Normal enemies and gears deal 30,
  boss melee 50; Muse HP is 200-300 depending on the character. [wiki: Baidu Baike; guide]
- Note types: small enemy (tap), large enemy (more damage), raider (jumps in from below), **gear / saw** (jump over
  or take damage and break the combo), **hammer** (swings from the top or bottom), **air+ground pair** (hit both),
  **ghost** (fades out; bonus only, no miss), **sheet music hold** (hit the star, hold to the end), **"!" mash**
  enemy, **blue eighth-note pickups** (+200 by touching, not a miss if skipped), **hearts** (heal). Bosses attack
  from the right. [wiki: wikiwiki.jp Muse Dash Wiki extracts; Stages page]
- **Fever**: hits fill a gauge; in Fever enemies are worth x1.5; later versions allow manual Fever. [wiki; review]
- End-of-song **rank** from accuracy and combo; the result screen shows the Muse. [review]
- The Muse's strike animation follows the chart. [GCores feature]

## Other runners
- **BIT.TRIP RUNNER** (Gaijin Games): auto-run; jump, slide, kick, later block/spring; obstacles placed on the
  beat; the music gains layers as obstacles are cleared; no checkpoints. Sequels Runner2 / Runner3 keep it.
  [review: GamesRadar, Giant Bomb, Pocket Gamer; MobyGames]
- **CookieRun: OvenBreak** (Devsisters): auto-run; jump, double jump, slide; collect jellies; obstacles; power-ups
  clear obstacles; speed rises over time. [store; fan wiki]
- Other rhythm runners: Geometry Dash, Melody's Escape, Hextech Mayhem, Lost in Harmony. [store / listings]

## VTuber stream framing
- Typical gaming layout: the game capture is the main layer, the avatar sits in a corner over it (placed so it
  never covers the game's own HUD), chat and alerts on top; 1920x1080 canvas. [guide: voxbooster, OBS overlay
  guides]

## Rules adopted for the PV (v3)
1. XIYIN is a VTuber streaming a rhythm runner; the runner is **her avatar in the game** (pixel chibi), drawn in
   true side view, **facing right and looking where she runs**. Only the VTuber (HD anime model, the on-model
   stickers) looks at the viewer.
2. The avatar runs constantly at a fixed screen x on the left; the scroll never stops. The only freeze is when the
   VTuber pauses the game (the whole game image stops; resume with a 3-2-1 count).
3. Enemies arrive from the right on the beat chart, in the air or ground lane. On each hit the avatar attacks:
   ground = punch, air = jump + swipe, then she falls back unless another air note follows. Gears on the ground are
   jumped over. Pickups: blue eighth notes, hearts.
4. A note that reaches her unhit = contact damage: hurt flinch, blink, HP down, combo reset, MISS.
5. HP bar, score, combo, Fever gauge; Fever for the final chorus; result screen with rank and her portrait.
6. Opening: her streaming room with the computer; ending: results, goodbye, PC shutdown screen.
