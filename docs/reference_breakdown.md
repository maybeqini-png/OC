# Reference MV breakdown (for 「余裕だもん」)

Source: YouTube storyboard frames (the official preview thumbnails, about one frame per 1-2 s), fetched
2026-10-22. Full video downloads are blocked in this environment (YouTube bot check + proxy), so cut
timings are approximate and fast cuts under ~1 s can be missed. REFLECT (Gawr Gura) and the 悠針れい teaser
could not be fetched. Evidence level: observed on storyboard frames unless marked otherwise.

## 1. 粛聖!! ロリ神レクイエム☆ (しぐれうい) — Ci_zad39Uhw, ~150 frames
Credits (KAI-YOU, verified): illustration & animation ががめ, movie 鬼灯わらべ, illustration also しぐれうい.
- **Character**: one hand-drawn animator, chibi-leaning proportions; a short dance loop is reused all
  through the MV on purpose (the copyable dance went viral). A second set: bust "sticker" drawings with a
  thick white outline, hands near the face, used for 5-6 consecutive beats with only text/background changing.
- **Backgrounds**: almost never a real place. Neon purple radial bursts, flat pastel colour fields
  (yellow, mint, pink) with halftone, black with red horror text, soft bright blur for calm lines.
  The background colour flips every 1-2 beats.
- **Typography is the main visual**: giant katakana/kanji walls behind the character (ロリ, 神降臨,
  バイバイ, 鳴らせば), mid-size kanji words beside the sticker bust (泥水, 妄想, 法廷で), small chat-like text
  scrolling (fans' voices), red serif text for dark lines, book-style vertical text on paper for the bridge.
- **Structure / colour script**: soft blurred intro → neon dance + text walls → sticker-bust word gags →
  dark chat section → orange fire chorus with face-reaction gags → pink-ghost horror bit → bridge (beam
  battle with a giant monster, then a calm white "angel" version with vertical serif lyrics) → B/W manga
  close-ups → neon dance finale → credits on black.
- **Lesson**: repetition of the *character drawing* is fine when it is a designed motif (dance, sticker
  bust) and everything around it changes on the beat. Draft 1 repeated a still with a slow zoom — the opposite.

## 2. ひっひっふー (しぐれうい) — VXUbEH0DvSM, ~90 frames
Credits (end card): Music じん, Movie Hurray!.
- **One colour theme**: red + white (+ small yellow/teal accents) for the whole MV; everything is recoloured
  into it, which is what makes very different shots feel like one work.
- **One main set (her desk/room) seen from many angles**: fisheye, top-down, low angle, extreme
  perspective hand toward the camera. The set is reused, the camera never repeats.
- Bold display typography (white on red, red on cream), English/Japanese mixed; UI gags (SYSTEM IS DOWN
  screen, comment cards, browser windows); short cutaway gags with side characters.
- **Lesson**: a single environment can carry an MV if each cut has a new angle and a clear graphic idea.

## 3. うみゃうにゃはっぴーらいふ (水宮枢) — _tVZp3ufOOs, ~99 frames (1 per ~2 s)
- **Closest match to our OC**: moe, pastel sky blue + pink + cream, blue-haired character with a
  sleep-mask motif. Many illustrators are credited (normal and chibi styles).
- **Sticker collage system**: characters as thick-white-outline stickers inside decorated frames (lace,
  ribbons, candy icons, scrapbook paper, pixel-heart borders); normal-proportion illustrations and chibi
  stickers mixed in one frame; big rounded outlined typography (white fill, blue stroke) as part of the layout.
- **Variety**: roughly 30-40 distinct drawings (poses, expressions, chibi gags, food props, a cartoon
  landscape with dialogue subtitles, an all-over pattern of chibi faces), each held 1-4 s.
- **Lesson**: the "sticker" look is not cheap by itself — it is cheap when stickers float over unrelated
  realistic plates. Here they sit in a designed, on-palette collage with frames and typography.

## 4. GREATEST (音乃瀬奏) — PeitgWu6Yj8, ~150 frames
- Fully animated TV-anime-quality MV (studio work): every cut a new composition, a fantasy journey
  (red room → teal sky → magic watch → green forest → mushroom forest with doors).
- Strong colour script per section (red → teal → green), character acting in every cut.
- **Lesson**: out of reach for frame-by-frame animation here, but the rules carry over: no repeated
  composition, a colour per section, props as story devices.

## 5. メズマライザー (32ki / サツキ) — 19y8YTbvri8, ~80 frames
Credits (KAI-YOU, verified): movie by channel.
- One rigid symmetric layout for almost the whole MV: two characters in vertical side panels, a centre
  icon (sun, spiral, quiz object), small subtitles. Backgrounds flip red / blue / black / white on the beat,
  poses change every beat, confetti and spiral variations build intensity.
- Deliberately simple, flat drawing style; consistency through simplicity.
- **Lesson**: a fixed layout + per-beat pose and colour flips reads as a hypnotic system, not as repetition.

## What this means for our PV
1. Build a **visual system**, not a slideshow: a few layout families that recur with per-beat variation.
2. **Typography is a primary visual** (text walls, word stickers, chat voices), not captions.
3. **One colour theme** (her navy/cobalt/sky + cream/white; coral for feelings, lemon for gags), every asset
   recoloured into it.
4. **Many distinct drawings** (target 40+ character drawings: normal + chibi), each on a plain background so
   the character can be checked and colour-locked, then placed into designed frames; plus ~10 full scenes for
   story beats (room, stage, desk), each seen from a new angle.
5. Repetition only as a **designed motif** (the めっ boop pose, a copyable hook pose), always with the
   surroundings changing on the beat.
